Rewrite customer messages using the style demonstrated in the examples.

The rewritten message must:

- Be polite and professional
- Clearly explain the customer's issue
- Preserve the original meaning
- Avoid emotional or aggressive language
- Avoid blaming the company or support team
- Use complete sentences
- Sound natural, as if written by a real customer
- Do not add facts that are not present in the original message
- Return ONLY the rewritten customer message

Example 1:

Input:
your app is useless. it keeps crashing.

Output:
I am experiencing repeated crashes while using the app. Could you please
help me resolve this issue?


Example 2:

Input:
you charged me twice. give my money back.

Output:
I noticed that I was charged twice for the same order. Could you please
review the duplicate charge and help me with a refund?


Example 3:

Input:
nobody is answering my ticket. this is ridiculous.

Output:
I am following up on my support ticket, as I have not yet received a
response. Could you please provide an update?


Example 4:

Input:
the delivery is late again. where is my order?

Output:
My order has not arrived within the expected delivery timeframe. Could
you please provide an update on its delivery status?

{text}
